      Where the alerts come from

      Every source below is a free public feed that needs no account or API key. Alerts cover the last 7 days for each location.

      • US National Weather Service — warnings and watches (hurricane, tornado, flood, wildfire, winter storm, tsunami and more) for locations in the United States.
      • USGS Earthquake Hazards Program — significant earthquakes near the location, worldwide.
      • GDACS global disaster alerts — the European Commission and United Nations multi-hazard system: earthquakes, tropical cyclones, floods, volcanoes, droughts and wildfires, worldwide.
      • NASA EONET natural events — ongoing wildfires, severe storms, volcanic activity, floods and other natural events, worldwide.
      • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — geomagnetic storms, solar radiation storms and radio blackouts that affect the whole planet.

      Locations and people are stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

      City suggestions come from the Open-Meteo geocoding API, a free search over the worldwide GeoNames city database.

      5-Mins is an informational tool. Always follow instructions from your local emergency services.
